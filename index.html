01 / The idea

Your business shouldn't depend on copy, paste and follow-up.

Repetitive admin quietly costs businesses hours every week: moving data between spreadsheets, sending the same emails, updating records, preparing reports and chasing routine tasks.

Praxis turns those processes into connected workflows that run with less human intervention — while keeping your existing tools at the centre of the system.
